What Is This Exam

A developer-focused GenAI certification

AIP-C01 is aimed at people who build GenAI applications on AWS — not at data scientists who train models. It tests practical, production-oriented skills across the full lifecycle of a GenAI application.

The exam validates your ability to

  • Design solutions using vector stores, Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG), and knowledge bases
  • Integrate foundation models (FMs) into applications and business workflows
  • Apply prompt engineering and prompt management techniques
  • Implement agentic AI solutions and tool integrations
  • Optimize GenAI applications for cost, performance, and business value
  • Implement security, governance, and Responsible AI practices
  • Troubleshoot, monitor, and evaluate FMs for quality and safety

Target candidate

  • 2+ years building production-grade applications on AWS or open-source tech
  • General AI/ML or data engineering experience
  • 1+ year of hands-on experience implementing GenAI solutions

Out of scope

  • Model development & training
  • Advanced ML techniques
  • Data & feature engineering

How To Prepare

A four-stage study plan

This is a hands-on, scenario-heavy exam — reading alone won't cut it. Pair every concept with a small build.

1

Learn the fundamentals

Work through the official AIP-C01 exam guide, AWS Skill Builder's GenAI learning plan, the Bedrock user guide, and the AWS Well-Architected Generative AI Lens.

2

Build one project per domain

Stand up a real RAG app with Knowledge Bases + OpenSearch, an agent with Bedrock Agents, a guardrail policy, and a deployed API — don't just read about them.

3

Go deep on security & governance

Domain 3 is scenario-based: least-privilege IAM, KMS encryption, PII redaction, audit logging with CloudTrail, and Responsible AI guardrails come up constantly.

4

Practice under exam conditions

Take full-length, timed practice exams. Review every wrong answer against the task statement it maps to, and weight your final review toward Domains 1 & 2.
